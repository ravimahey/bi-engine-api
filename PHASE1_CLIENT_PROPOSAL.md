# Ordaraa Spend Intelligence — Phase 1

**What this phase delivers, and what to expect.**

---

## Where we're starting from

Your business's transaction data (from QuickBooks) is already connecting
and syncing into Ordaraa. Phase 1 turns that data into four practical
tools an owner can actually use — not a big analytics platform, four
focused features that each answer one real question you'd otherwise have
to dig for by hand.

## What you get in this phase

### 1. Always-Accurate Spend Totals

Your dashboard shows what you actually spent — total spend, spend by
category, spend by supplier — calculated from **every transaction on
record**, not just the ones currently on screen. Change the date range and
every number updates to match, instantly and correctly.

*Why it matters:* you can trust the number at the top of the screen is the
real number, every time, no matter how much history is behind it.

### 2. Duplicate Invoice / Expense Detection

If the same supplier, same date, and same amount shows up twice — a
classic sign of a bill entered or paid twice — Ordaraa flags it
automatically and shows you both records side by side so you can confirm
before it costs you twice.

*Example:* ABC Supplies, $2,150, same date, appears in your records twice
→ flagged as a possible duplicate, not silently counted as $4,300 of real
spend.

### 3. Money Leak Alerts (Cost & Supplier Increases)

Ordaraa watches for two of the most common ways money quietly leaks out of
a business:

- **A cost category creeping up** — e.g. packaging spend going from
  $8,000 to $11,000.
- **A supplier's spend climbing** — e.g. one supplier going from $20,000
  to $26,000 over a period.

Each alert comes with the *why* behind it: the real transactions that
drove the change, and — importantly — whether the increase is because
prices went up or because you simply bought more. We never label a normal
growth in volume as "wasted money"; the alert tells you honestly which one
it is.

### 4. Missing / Incorrect Transaction Check

A simple, always-on data-quality check: transactions missing a supplier,
missing a date, or with an amount that doesn't make sense are flagged so
you know your dashboard is built on clean data — instead of silently
producing a wrong number.

---

## How we keep every number trustworthy

This is the part worth understanding, because it's what makes the whole
system dependable rather than "an AI's best guess":

**Every number you see is calculated directly from your database, across
your full transaction history — not estimated, not sampled, not eyeballed
by AI.** The same data will always produce the same total, the same
duplicate flag, the same spend-increase alert, no matter how large your
transaction history grows.

AI's role sits *on top of* those already-correct numbers, not underneath
them: once the real calculation is done, AI turns it into a plain-English
explanation — *"Packaging spend rose $3,000, mostly driven by ABC
Supplies"* — instead of a raw table. AI explains and summarizes; it never
invents a supplier, a transaction, or a dollar figure, and it never
overrides what the actual calculation found. If asked something the data
can't support, it says so rather than guessing.

## What this phase does not include

To keep this focused and deliverable quickly, Phase 1 deliberately leaves
out:

- Full accounting/bookkeeping (general ledger, bank reconciliation)
- Automatically acting on a finding (e.g. auto-switching suppliers) —
  every action stays a human decision
- A general-purpose AI chatbot — only the four features above
- Forecasting or predicting future prices
- A native mobile app

These are natural next-phase candidates once Phase 1 is proven, not gaps
in this delivery.

## Delivery plan

Because your transaction data is already syncing, Phase 1 skips the
data-onboarding work and goes straight to building on top of it.

| Stage | Focus | You'll be able to |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Data-quality check + accurate spend totals | See a dashboard where every total is correct and any bad data is flagged |
| Stage 2 | Duplicate detection + money-leak alerts | See flagged duplicates and cost/supplier increase alerts, each with the evidence behind it |
| Stage 3 | Review, plain-English explanations, polish | Read a clear explanation for every alert, and confirm accuracy against your real records |

*(Planning estimate, not a fixed date — will be firmed up once work
starts, in line with normal delivery practice.)*

## What "done" looks like

Phase 1 is complete when: you can open the dashboard and see spend totals
that are correct across your entire history, every duplicate expense is
caught before it's double-counted, every meaningful cost or supplier
increase is flagged with the real transactions behind it, and any
incomplete or incorrect transaction is called out — all without anyone
manually fixing data behind the scenes to make a demo look right.
